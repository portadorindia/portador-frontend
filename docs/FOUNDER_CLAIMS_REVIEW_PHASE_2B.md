## High-priority FAQ claim groups

### 1. Guaranteed delivery and fixed time promises

Representative source questions: 8, 106, and 332.

Risk: wording includes time-definite guarantees and may imply PORTADOR guarantees outcomes controlled by airlines, airports, weather, customs, or other third parties.

Founder decision needed: identify whether any written product-level SLA exists, its exclusions, eligible routes, remedies, and approval authority.

### 2. Exact delivery timelines and lane promises

Representative source questions: 1-7, 47, 84, 137, 213, 248, 250-252, 258, 261, 271, and 278.

Risk: answers state specific 6-12 hour, same-day, overnight, or 24-48 hour outcomes without always stating route, pickup, cargo, acceptance, and disruption conditions.

Founder decision needed: approve a standard feasibility disclaimer and confirm which timelines are marketing guidance versus contractual commitments.

### 3. Airline cutoffs and terminal timing

Representative source questions: 2, 10, 250, 251, and 271.

Risk: fixed morning or 11:00 AM cutoffs may change by airport, carrier, cargo type, documentation, and day of operation.

Founder decision needed: confirm whether cutoff information should be removed from evergreen content or presented only as booking-time guidance.

### 4. GPS, live tracking, integrations, and real-time visibility

Representative source questions: 8, 31, 36, 41, 237, 282, and 375.

Risk: answers refer to live GPS links, maps, API webhooks, ERP integration, and real-time tracking that may not be available for every service or shipment leg.

Founder decision needed: document the exact PORTADOR-OPS milestones, GPS availability, notification channels, API availability, and service-level exceptions.

### 5. Proof of Delivery timing and evidence

Representative source questions: 34, 35, 191, 192, 200, 244, and 379.

Risk: answers may imply a fixed POD format, immediate timing, photograph, time stamp, or electronic-signature capability across every service.

Founder decision needed: confirm POD formats and timing by service, partner, and destination.

### 6. Insurance, liability, and claims

Representative source questions: 25-28, 105, 183-192, 230, 231, 302, 325, and 346-353.

Risk: coverage descriptions, exclusions, claim values, liability caps, and settlement expectations may differ by policy, carrier, commodity, packaging, declared value, and written acceptance.

Founder decision needed: align all answers with the final Booking & Refund Policy, Terms & Conditions, approved insurer language, and written risk-selection process.

### 7. Pricing, surcharges, fees, and savings claims

Representative source questions: 21-24, 29, 30, 72, 79, 198, 203, 207, 229, 235, 236, 256, 270, 304, 341, and 344.

Risk: answers may promise included fees, no extra charge, fixed pricing, percentage savings, free storage, or standard quote components that vary by shipment.

Founder decision needed: approve a single pricing disclosure explaining chargeable weight, handling, pickup/delivery, taxes, special handling, storage, and third-party charges.

### 8. Airline acceptance and regulated cargo

Representative source questions: 89, 99, 128, 137, 145, 148, 217-226, 280, 294, 314, and 360.

Risk: some answers describe items as universally allowed or prohibited. Acceptance can depend on classification, quantity, packing instruction, MSDS/declaration, equipment configuration, carrier rules, and law.

Founder decision needed: have a qualified DG/compliance reviewer approve the wording and ensure the Restricted Goods page remains controlling.

### 9. International customs and import/export support

Representative source questions: 107 and international/import/export questions across the global-shipping sections.

Risk: content may imply customs clearance, duty treatment, documentation handling, or acceptance beyond PORTADOR's contracted scope.

Founder decision needed: define countries, commodities, incoterm responsibilities, customs-broker scope, duties/taxes responsibility, and restricted destinations.

### 10. Service availability and third-party access

Representative source questions: 47, 84, 137, 216, 248-258, 271, 332, and 344.

Risk: airport pickup, lost-and-found coordination, hotel pickup, same-day availability, OBC, and special access depend on permissions, timing, cargo eligibility, and local serviceability.

Founder decision needed: approve standard wording that support is checked case by case and is not confirmed until accepted in writing.
